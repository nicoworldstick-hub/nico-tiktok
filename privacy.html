Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

"Nico Uploader TikTok" is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos from the animated series "Nico Last Light" to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we access

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API only to authorize the owner's own account and to upload videos the owner created. It accesses basic profile information (display name) of that single account.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold, or published. We do not collect data from other users and do not use cookies or analytics.

Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Revoking access removes the tokens' validity.
